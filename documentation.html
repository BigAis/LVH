Installation Guide

Welcome to Lizards VS Humans! This guide will walk you through the installation process step by step.

Digital Platforms

Lizards VS Humans is available on various digital platforms. Follow the platform-specific instructions below:

Steam

  1. Open the Steam client and log in to your account
  2. Search for "Lizards VS Humans" in the store
  3. Purchase the game or claim it if it's already in your library
  4. Click the "Install" button and select your installation location
  5. Wait for the download and installation to complete

Epic Games Store

  1. Open the Epic Games launcher and log in to your account
  2. Search for "Lizards VS Humans" in the store
  3. Purchase the game or claim it if it's already in your library
  4. Click the "Install" button and select your installation location
  5. Wait for the download and installation to complete

Xbox

  1. Turn on your Xbox console and navigate to the Store
  2. Search for "Lizards VS Humans"
  3. Purchase the game or download it if you already own it
  4. The game will automatically install to your default storage location
  5. You can monitor the download progress from the "Queue" section

PlayStation

  1. Turn on your PlayStation console and navigate to the PlayStation Store
  2. Search for "Lizards VS Humans"
  3. Purchase the game or download it if you already own it
  4. The game will automatically install to your default storage location
  5. You can monitor the download progress from the "Downloads" section
Note: The game requires approximately 50GB of free space. Make sure you have sufficient storage available before starting the installation.

Advanced Movement Techniques

Mastering these advanced movements can give you a significant advantage in combat:

Wall Running

  1. Approach a wall at an angle while sprinting
  2. Jump toward the wall and hold forward
  3. Your character will automatically begin wall running
  4. Jump again to leap off the wall

Slide

  1. While sprinting, press the crouch button
  2. Your character will slide in the direction you're moving
  3. You can shoot during the slide
  4. Jump at the end of a slide for extra momentum
TIP: Humans have a longer slide duration, while Lizards have faster slide speed.

Ledge Grab

  1. Jump towards a ledge or edge
  2. Your character will automatically grab it if within range
  3. Press jump again to climb up
  4. Press crouch to drop from the ledge
