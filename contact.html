FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How can I report a bug in your games?

You can report bugs through our support portal, which you can access via your game account or by emailing support@projectallecc.com. Please include as much information as possible, including screenshots or video recordings of the issue.

Do you offer game testing opportunities?

Yes, we regularly recruit players for beta testing upcoming games and major updates. Sign up for our newsletter or create an account on our website to be notified about testing opportunities.

What platforms do your games support?

Our games are currently available on PC (via Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox One. Mobile versions of select titles are in development.

How can I apply for a job at Project Allecc?

We post all open positions on the Careers section of our website. You can also send your portfolio and resume to careers@projectallecc.com even if there are no current openings that match your skills.

Do you offer internships or student programs?

Yes, we offer summer internships and year-round co-op programs for students pursuing degrees in game development, computer science, animation, and other relevant fields. Check our Careers page for current opportunities.

Can I use your games for content creation?

Absolutely! We encourage content creators to stream, record, and share gameplay from our titles. You're welcome to monetize this content on platforms like YouTube and Twitch, as long as you're following their respective terms of service.
