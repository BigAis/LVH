OUR STORY

Project Allecc was founded in 2023 by two passionate gamers with a vision to create innovative gaming experiences that challenge conventions and push boundaries.

What started as a small indie studio in Athens, Greece, has grown into a dedicated team of developers, artists, and designers united by our love for games and commitment to quality.

Our name "Allecc" represents our core values: Artistry, Learning, Legacy, Excellence, Creativity, and Community. These principles guide every project we undertake and every game we create.

OUR MISSION

We believe games are more than just entertainment—they're powerful mediums for storytelling, connection, and expression. Our mission is to create memorable gaming experiences that resonate with players on both emotional and intellectual levels.

We strive to balance innovative gameplay mechanics with compelling narratives, ensuring every title we release offers something unique to the gaming landscape.

Community involvement is central to our development process. We actively engage with players throughout each stage of creation, from concept to post-launch support, ensuring our games evolve with the community's feedback.
